Accepted payment methods

Payment methods at Pandora
Method How it works Extra fee
Mobile money Pay with the number shown at checkout and enter the confirmation code. None
Debit or credit card Visa and Mastercard are accepted through our secure payment page. None
Cash on delivery Pay the rider when your order arrives (Nairobi only). KES 100
Bank transfer Send the amount to our account and email the receipt. We ship once it clears. None

How to pay in four steps

  1. Add your items to the cart and choose Checkout.
  2. Enter your delivery address and phone number.
  3. Pick a payment method from the list above.
  4. Confirm the payment and wait for your order confirmation message.

Payment questions

Is my payment information safe?
Yes. Card details are entered on an encrypted page and are never stored on our site.
Can I get a refund?
Returns are accepted within 14 days. Refunds go back to the method you paid with within 5 working days.
I paid but did not get a confirmation
Wait 10 minutes, then contact us with your payment code.
